## Local preparation

The source images were downloaded as high-quality JPEGs with these query
parameters: `?auto=format&fit=max&w=3200&q=95&fm=jpg`. Square crops were selected
from those downloaded dimensions, in pixels:

| Local basename | Downloaded dimensions | Crop x, y, width, height | Composition retained |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `engineering-bridge` | 3200 × 2136 | 850, 0, 2136, 2136 | Steel trusses and bridge mechanism |
| `calculus-integral` | 3200 × 2124 | 550, 0, 2124, 2124 | Central integral, lower limit and writing hand |
| `linear-equations` | 3200 × 2133 | 533, 0, 2133, 2133 | In-focus lower linear equation and surrounding system |

Each crop was resized locally to 1600 × 1600 and 800 × 800 with Sharp, then encoded
as WebP at quality 84, effort 6. Original colours and photographed mathematical
content were preserved. No generated content, recolouring, retouching, added
formulae or stamped course labels were used. Responsive frames may crop the
photographs further for their available dimensions.

All six files are served locally; no third-party image request is made at runtime.
This file covers image assets only. Existing Giats, Kairo and Atelier North code
credits and licence notices are retained separately.
