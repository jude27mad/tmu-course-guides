# Course artwork credits

These are existing scientific satellite artworks from the U.S. Geological Survey's
**Earth as Art 1** collection. They are used for their spatial compositions, not as
literal course illustrations or photographs of Toronto Metropolitan University.
The images depict the locations identified below; none depicts TMU.

Each asset's own USGS source page was checked on **2026-10-15** and explicitly
states **“Sources/Usage: Public Domain.”** Permission was verified separately for
all three images; no template's code licence was used to infer image rights.

## Preparation and credit policy

The source images are 7904 × 8512 pixel posters. Only the 7296 × 7296 pixel
scientific image area was extracted, starting at x=304, y=760. The surrounding
poster titles, institutional identifiers and borders are excluded. Images were
resized to 1600 × 1600 and 800 × 800 and encoded locally using Sharp WebP quality
78, effort 6, without changing the source colours. Responsive display crops are
centred. No external image service or third-party runtime request is required.

USGS asks users to provide proper source credit; the credits above fulfil that
request. The verified USGS policy also cautions that some third-party material on
its website is copyrighted, which is why the individual Public Domain notices
above were checked:

<https://www.usgs.gov/information-policies-and-instructions/copyrights-and-credits>

Collection and institutional creator:
<https://www.usgs.gov/centers/eros/earth-art-1>.

This file covers the image assets only. Giats, Kairo and Atelier North code notices
are retained separately alongside the application's licence documentation.
