        Rules & help

        Which quiz-drop rule applies?

        The Fall 2026 outline lists four quizzes, one lowest quiz dropped, and a 15% category. The September 22 Quiz 1 announcement confirms the lowest-grade drop. The FAQ’s “two out of ten” wording conflicts with those documents and is not used here.

        Assessment rules

        • At least 50% of total course marks is required to pass.
        • Quizzes and exams are individual work, closed book, with no calculator, formula sheet or other aids.
        • Four lab quizzes share 15% of the course grade. The lowest quiz is dropped. No quiz make-ups.
        • Recommended homework is practice and is not submitted for grading.

        Source: Course Management Form and course FAQ.

        If you miss an assessment

        Your first missed lab quiz is handled by the dropped lowest grade; the course announcements say not to submit an ACR for that first missed quiz.

        For further missed quizzes, contact your instructor or TA and follow the current Academic Consideration procedure. There is no lab-quiz make-up.

        For a missed midterm or final, contact your instructor promptly and follow the official ACR process. Make-ups require a valid, approved reason.

        Source: Course Management Form, missed-evaluation rules, and quiz announcements.

        Regrading & communication

        Request regrading or recalculation within 10 working days after the graded assessment is returned, subject to the current University and course procedure. The whole assessment may be regraded and the mark may increase or decrease.

        Use your official TMU email, include your name, student number, course and section. Check D2L first and allow at least one business day for a response.

        Source: Course Management Form and FAQ.
