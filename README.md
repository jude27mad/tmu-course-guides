# TMU Course Guides

An unofficial course companion for Toronto Metropolitan University students. It brings deadlines, requirements, checklists and your own progress into one place, and works on phones, tablets and desktops.

**[Open the website →](https://jude27mad.github.io/tmu-course-guides/)**

No account, installation or download is needed.

> **The current design is live at the main address.** Links to the previous version's pages and to the earlier `/next/` preview open the matching page of the current design, and saved progress carries over. The previous version's files stay in this repository for reference but are no longer published.

## Getting started

1. **Start at the Desk.** The home page shows the next deadline across all your courses, with a live countdown, and a card for each course.
2. **Open a guide.** Choose **Open guide** next to a course in the Courses list, tap a course card, or use the **Menu**.
3. **Set your section at the top.** Choose your section (and your Innovation Challenge group in CEN100). Quiz dates, lab times, rooms and contacts then match your section. The choice is remembered in this browser.
4. **Open your work.** Each guide starts with a compact **Work** list. Use **Open**, **All** or **Done**, then open an assignment for its dates, requirements, checklist and notes. **Back** restores your filter and place in the list. Past-due and undated work stay visible.
5. **Check the schedule.** **Schedule** has an agenda for phones and a calendar on wider screens. Both open the same assignment details. **All dates** includes earlier deadlines. Times are in Toronto; a quiz week or unconfirmed date is labelled as such.
6. **Record progress in the details.** Tick preparation steps as you finish them, and mark each submission separately under **My plan & completion**. Math guides also have **Study** for topic material; **Course info** holds grading, contacts and policies.
7. **Calculate your grade.** **Grades** has a grade calculator with the course's own weights. Enter marks as a percentage or a score (17 / 20) to see your current average and letter, what you have secured, the best grade still possible, and the average you need on the rest for each letter.

## Finding things

- **Search** finds courses, assessments, weeks, topics and actions. It is in the top bar on larger screens and in the **Menu** on phones. On a keyboard, press **Ctrl K** (or **⌘ K** on a Mac).
- The **Menu** has every page, the next few deadlines across your courses, **Pause motion** and links to official sources such as D2L.
- Links to a single item, such as those in the Menu and in search results, open that item's details directly.

## Checklists, records and notes

These controls are separate on purpose:

- **Ticking a checklist step** tracks your preparation. It does not mark the item complete.
- **Submission/completion boxes** record that you have completed or submitted each required part. An assignment stays Open until every submission is marked. Clear a box to undo a record. The website does not submit work or confirm anything with the course.
- **Notes and target dates** are private reminders that stay on the item.

## Grade calculator

Each guide's **Grades** tab uses the same weights as **Course info → Grading**:

- **Marks.** Type a percentage, or a score and its total (17 / 20). Typing 17/20 in the first box fills both. Leave a box empty for work that isn't marked yet; an empty box is different from 0.
- **Your standing.** The current average is your grade on the work marked so far; keep it on the rest and it becomes your final grade. (In MTH140 and MTH141 you can finish a little higher, because a low quiz that counts now may be dropped later; the calculator says so when that happens.) **Secured** is what you have already earned even with 0 on everything left, **Best possible** assumes 100 on everything left, and **Still to be marked** is the share of the course not yet decided.
- **On phones** the marks come first, with your average and letter kept in view above them as you scroll; the full summary and **What do I need?** follow the marks. On wider screens the summary sits beside the marks.
- **What do I need?** Each letter shows the average you need on everything still to be marked, or whether it is already secured or out of reach. Choose one for a sentence that names the item when only one is left, such as the final exam.
- **Dropped quizzes.** In MTH140 and MTH141 the lowest lab quiz is dropped, as the course policies state. A quiz shows as Dropped once more quizzes are entered than count.
- **Letters.** TMU's undergraduate scale, with the university's rounding: the final percentage is rounded to a whole number before it becomes a letter, so 79.5% is an A-. Some programs, including engineering, require more than a D- in particular courses.

The calculator is an estimate from the weights in the guide. Your course outline and the grades your instructors post are what count.

## Guide tools

Use **Guide tools** beside D2L at the top of each guide:

- **Download calendar (.ics):** adds upcoming course dates to your calendar app. In MTH140 and MTH141, lab quiz dates are included once you choose your section. The file is a snapshot; it does not update itself when dates change.
- **Export my progress / Import a backup:** saves this course's ticks, completion records, notes and target dates to a file, and adds them to the same course in another browser or on another device. Section and group choices are not included.
- **Clear my ticks** (CEN100): confirm to remove CEN100's checklist ticks. A confirmation appears when they are cleared; completion records, notes, targets and section choices stay saved. Untick a completion record in its assignment to undo it.
- **Course updates:** changes to the course's dates, coverage and grading. A badge appears when there is something new. Changes to the website itself are on the **Website updates** page, linked from the menu and the footer.

## Saving your progress

Your sections, ticks, records, notes, target dates and calculator marks are saved **in the browser you are using**, not in an account. Each course keeps its own progress, and nothing syncs between devices or browsers.

To move to another browser or device, export a backup from each guide, import it there, and choose your section again. Backups don't include calculator marks. Keep backup files private, as they can contain your notes. Clearing your browser data, or switching devices without a backup, leaves your saved progress behind.

Progress saved by earlier versions of the website carries over automatically.

## Motion and accessibility

- Course tabs and utility controls use a subtle glass finish; instructions and notes have solid backgrounds. **Pause motion** makes these controls solid, stops animations, shows finished photographs, removes the pointer effect and skips page transitions. The preference stays on in this browser. Mouse-wheel scrolling is still smoothed. Your device's reduced-motion setting has the same effect; reduced transparency and increased contrast also make the controls solid where supported.
- If the effects feel slow on an older phone, turn on **Pause motion**.
- Pinch zoom, text selection, keyboard navigation and visible focus all work.
- Without JavaScript, course information, rules, grading and contacts are still readable; deadlines, countdowns, checklists and item details need JavaScript.
- The new design uses a light appearance only.

## Privacy

The website has no analytics, tracking cookies or session recording, and it makes no requests to other websites. Fonts and graphics are served from the website itself. See the [Privacy page](https://jude27mad.github.io/tmu-course-guides/privacy/) for details, including how GitHub Pages hosting works.

## Important

This is an **unofficial student-built companion**, not a replacement for D2L or announcements from the teaching team. Always check official sources for changes. The website does not access your D2L account, submit assignments, verify your records or update calendars you have already imported.
