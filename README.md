# TMU Course Guides

An unofficial, browser-based course companion for Toronto Metropolitan University. The website brings deadlines, requirements, study references, and personal progress tracking into one place, with a layout designed for phones, tablets, and desktops.

**[Open the website →](https://jude27mad.github.io/tmu-course-guides/)**

No account, installation, or download is needed.

## Getting started

1. **Choose a guide.** Start from the [home page](https://jude27mad.github.io/tmu-course-guides/) or open [CEN100](https://jude27mad.github.io/tmu-course-guides/cen100/), [MTH140](https://jude27mad.github.io/tmu-course-guides/mth140/), or [MTH141](https://jude27mad.github.io/tmu-course-guides/mth141/) directly.
2. **Set your section.** Select your course section to show relevant details. The CEN100 guide also supports an Innovation Challenge group selection. Your choices are remembered in this browser.
3. **See what's next.** Use the upcoming-deadline area and the **This week's work** shortcut to find work that needs attention. Open a card to read its details.
4. **Find things quickly.** Use **Search**, the guide's navigation tabs, and shortcuts to contacts or D2L instead of scrolling through the whole page.
5. **Track your work.** Tick preparation checklists, record an item as completed or submitted, and add private notes or personal target dates where available. These are separate controls: ticking a checklist does not submit work or confirm completion.
6. **Take your plan with you.** Use the guide tools to export dates to a calendar or save a progress backup. Calendar files are snapshots, not automatically syncing calendars.

## What the website offers

- **One starting point:** a course directory and an overview of upcoming work across your guides.
- **Personal planning:** upcoming work, checklists, completion records, notes, and study progress.
- **Fast navigation:** search, direct links to individual items, quick actions, and section-aware information.
- **Useful exports:** calendar downloads (`.ics`) and local progress backups (`.json`).
- **Accessible display options:** responsive layouts, automatic/light/dark appearance, keyboard navigation, and reduced-motion support.
- **In-page updates:** **What's changed?** near the bottom of each guide shows recent website changes.

## Saving and moving progress

Your section choices, notes, and progress are saved **in your current browser**, not in a website account. Each course keeps its own progress. There is no automatic sync between devices or browsers.

To keep a copy, use **Guide tools** or the guide's backup section to export your progress. Import the matching backup in the relevant guide when moving to another browser. Keep backup files private: they can contain your notes.

Clearing browser data or using a different device without importing a backup may leave your saved progress unavailable.

## Privacy and important notes

The website does not use site analytics, tracking cookies, or session recording. See the [Privacy page](https://jude27mad.github.io/tmu-course-guides/privacy.html) for details, including GitHub Pages hosting.

This is an **unofficial student-built companion**, not a replacement for D2L or announcements from the teaching team. Always check official sources for changes or confirmation. The site does not access your D2L account, submit assignments, verify your completion records, or automatically update calendars you have imported.
